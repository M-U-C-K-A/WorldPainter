# What's new

WorldPainter Modernised starts from WorldPainter 2.27, which supports Minecraft 1.20.5 to 26.x, and keeps everything it does. This page lists what it adds; each feature has a page of its own.

## Generate

- **[Node graph](node-graph.html)**: generate complete worlds from noise, erosion, rivers, lakes, masks and painters, with a live preview. 39 kinds of nodes, including [landforms](node-graph.html#landforms) (mountains, rolling hills, an island) ready made in one node, with the seldom used settings folded away, and examples to start from.
- **[Floating islands](tutorial-floating-islands.html)**: a node which builds islands in the sky.
- **[Edge blending](edge-blending.html)**: the map no longer ends in a cliff; its edges go over smoothly into the land around it.

## See

- **[Integrated 3D view](3d-view.html)**: in the main window, with five colour modes, a block by block mode, shaders with sunlight, skylight, shadows and moving water, and everything about the block under the mouse.
- **[Surroundings](surroundings-and-seeds.html)**: the biomes Minecraft will generate around the map, shown faintly around it.

## Build

- **[Paths](paths.html)**: roads through points you click, following the land, with bridges and tunnels.
- **[Seed search](surroundings-and-seeds.html#find-a-matching-seed)**: find a seed for which the land around the map matches its edges, and blend the edges into it.
- **[Packs](trees-and-materials.html#the-packs-panel)**: fourteen kinds of custom trees, twenty-four presets of flowers, plants, water plants and crops, and thirty-nine mixes of blocks (grass, dirt, stone, sand, cliffs…), ready to paint, with what each is made of on hover.
- **[Terrain stamps](interface.html#brushes)**: twenty-two brushes shaped like peaks, ranges, cirques, plateaus, volcanoes, valleys…

## Use

- **[Brush settings](interface.html#where-a-brush-applies)**: they stay when you switch tools, and can limit a brush by map selection, by height on a scale you can drag, by several terrain types or layers at once, and by slope, with undo.
- **[A new interface](interface.html)**: three [designs](interface.html#themes), Modern, Classic and Glass, light or dark, which change without restarting, with the Geist typeface, [areas as in Blender](interface.html#areas), which can be split, joined, moved and detached, with two maps or two 3D views at once if you like, [workspaces](interface.html#workspaces) for sculpting, painting or the 3D view, palettes which fill their panel, tools grouped by what they do, cards with an animation of each tool and a 3D preview of each brush on hover, rectangle selection, a dotted canvas, a unified title bar on macOS, and [settings windows](interface.html#settings-windows) with an illustrated banner and their settings grouped in cards.
- **Faster export**: about a third faster on large maps, with exactly the same blocks and light.
- **Map thumbnails**: on macOS the Finder shows the map of a world as the icon of its `.world` file, as it does for photos. Worlds get one when they are saved with this version.
- **Java 21**, native applications for macOS, Windows and Linux, and more memory for large maps.
